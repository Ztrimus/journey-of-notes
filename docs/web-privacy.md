# Browser practice privacy

The experimental [practice gym]({{ '/practice/' | relative_url }}) asks for microphone access only after you choose Start or Detect my Sa. Pitch analysis runs locally in your browser. The app does not record audio files, upload microphone samples, or use a cloud transcription service.

Practice records for piano, bansuri, guitar, Western flute and vocals stay in this browser’s local storage, with the latest 100 sessions retained. Export and import are optional manual backups. Clear History removes only this app’s saved practice records after confirmation. Clearing browser data can also delete those records. There is no account or cross-device sync.

GitHub Pages serves this website and may process normal website request information under [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Google Fonts requests load the typefaces, with system-font fallbacks. The app adds no analytics or advertising trackers.

Microphone pitch and response measurements are experimental and uncalibrated. They do not verify physical technique.

The [existing Android privacy policy]({{ '/privacy-policy.html' | relative_url }}) remains separate.
