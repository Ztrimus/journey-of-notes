# Privacy Policy — Journey of Notes

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

Journey of Notes ("the app") is developed by Saurabh Zinjad as a personal, non-commercial music practice tool.

---

## Information We Collect

The app does not require an account, ask for your name or email address, or track your location. The developer does not operate a server that receives your recordings or practice results. The app does not include advertising or developer-configured analytics or crash-reporting services.

## Microphone Access

The Bamboo Flute trainer requests microphone permission when you start listening. It uses the device's audio recording functionality and sound-level metering to detect note onsets for beat accuracy feedback.

This creates local recording files. Audio processing happens on your device; the app does not upload recordings to the developer or third parties. The current implementation stops recording when listening ends but does not explicitly delete the resulting files. Local files may remain until app storage is cleared or the operating system removes cached files.

You can deny or revoke microphone permission in your device settings. The piano prompt exercise does not require microphone access.

## Data Storage

Practice settings, note timestamps, and session results are processed on your device and are not uploaded by the app. The app does not provide cloud backup or account-based synchronization.

To remove local app data on Android, use your device's app settings to clear storage. This also resets local app state.

## Third-Party Services

The app makes the following internet requests:

- **GitHub:** On Android startup, the app requests the latest release metadata to check for updates. Choosing Install opens a GitHub download or release page in your browser. No recordings or practice results are included in the update request.
- **Freesound:** The flute metronome loads a click sound from `cdn.freesound.org`.

These services receive the network information needed to handle requests, including your IP address and standard request metadata. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies:

- [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
- [Freesound Privacy Policy](https://freesound.org/help/privacy/)

The privacy-policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which also receives network requests when you visit it.

## Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
