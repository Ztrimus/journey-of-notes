## Supplied PDFs and images

**Exercises:** letter-finding with right hand, then left, finger-number prompts, paired hands/octaves, and five-finger patterns. The finger diagram numbers outward from thumb=1. Source conflicts: level 4's B3/B5 example disagrees with its explanatory octave text; level 5's D–E–F–A–B example skips G although it resembles a contiguous five-finger run. The prototype uses an identified original C–D–E–F–G drill and defers simultaneous-hand scoring.

**Alankar & Warm up:** long Sa, ascending/descending swars, register movement, pulse, speed multiplication, handwritten alankars, printed pattern images, ornamentation and altered swars. Numeric labels do not always match written sequences: six notes next to an eight-swar ascent and twelve next to an ascent/descent. Build from explicit target sequences rather than those counts. Breath-retention timings and advanced ornament descriptions are source notes, not app-prescribed beginner requirements. Exact handwritten transcription remains future content review.

**Prompt images:** preserve hand, finger, note and octave; prioritize the note, spell out context, and add a highlighted key. Emoji and color should not be the only carriers of meaning.
