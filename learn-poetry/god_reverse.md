Vedanta gives you the backbone: it treats the self, ātman, as tied to brahman, and it also includes samsara, the cycle of rebirth and release. Hindu cosmology also treats time as cyclical, with yugas repeating across cosmic cycles rather than moving in a straight line forever. Advaita Vedanta pushes this further by saying brahman is the reality of all things and ātman is identical with brahman. (Encyclopedia Britannica)
